Positioning & risk

Alongside the payment rails, not against them

PayFlow doesn't compete with banks or card schemes — it sits on top of existing payment infrastructure and focuses on the layer merchants actually manage day to day.

  • Building more product than the market has asked for
  • Relying on payment providers without formal integration agreements
  • Underpricing support and implementation work
  • Handling financial data without strong security controls
